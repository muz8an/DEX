📖 Step-by-Step Android Setup (Tailscale)
1
Install Tailscale on Laptop and Android Phone
Download Tailscale from tailscale.com on Windows and from Google Play on your Android phone. Sign in with the same account on both devices.
2
Bind DEX to Network Interfaces
To allow incoming connections from your phone's Tailscale IP, start DEX with:
$env:DEX_HOST="0.0.0.0"; java -jar target\dex-ai-assistant-1.0.0-jar-with-dependencies.jar --server-only
3
Open the Paired Dashboard on your Phone
Scan the QR code above or open http://<LAPTOP-IP>:31416/?token=<YOUR-TOKEN> in Chrome on your phone. You can run all tools, open YouTube, request screenshots, and chat with DEX!
